## 🎯 Problem Statement

Retail stakeholders need consistent views of monthly revenue, high-value customers, and geographic demand.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Review monthly revenue trends for the period available in the report.
- Identify high-revenue customers and country-level patterns.
- Communicate business questions through a Power BI report.

## ✨ Features

- Monthly revenue trend view.
- Country and customer comparisons described in the project brief.
- Power BI report in PBIX format.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

1. Open Online Retail Store Analysis Dashboard.pbix in Power BI Desktop.
2. Inspect the data source, tables, date range, currency, and refresh status.
3. Review monthly revenue trends and confirm the reporting period.
4. Compare countries by revenue and quantity using the report's specified filters.
5. Review the top customers by revenue and geographic demand visuals.
6. Validate measures and filters, then document three to five findings from the actual report.
